# Privacy Policy — YouTube to Perplexity

**Last updated:** September 4, 2026

## Summary

YouTube to Perplexity does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the extension developer.

## What the extension does

When you click **Send to Perplexity**, the extension:

1. Reads the current YouTube video URL and title from the page you are viewing
2. Stores a one-time prompt locally in your browser (`browser.storage.local`) so it can be pasted into Perplexity
3. Optionally copies the prompt to your clipboard
4. Opens [perplexity.ai](https://www.perplexity.ai) in a new tab

## What we do not do

- We do not run analytics or tracking
- We do not send data to any server operated by the extension developer
- We do not sell or share user data
- We do not require an account

## Third-party services

When you use this extension, you are sent to **Perplexity AI**, which has its own privacy policy and terms. Any data you submit there is governed by Perplexity, not this extension.

## Data stored locally

Your settings (custom prompt templates, preferences) are saved via `browser.storage.sync` in Firefox and stay on your device.
